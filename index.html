Shikara on Dal Lake

Glide past floating gardens, lotus beds and lakeside markets in a wooden shikara, best experienced at first light.

Houseboat Stays

Sleep inside intricately carved cedar houseboats on the water, with traditional Kashmiri breakfast served fresh from shore.

Meadow Trekking

Traverse high-altitude trails through Betaab Valley, Baisaran near Pahalgam, or ridge routes around Gulmarg.

Alpine Skiing

From December to March, Gulmarg transforms into world-class ski terrain served by one of the highest gondolas on earth.

Food & Drink

Kashmiri hospitality is an art form best experienced around a laden table.

Beverage

Kahwa

A fragrant green tea brewed with whole cardamom, cinnamon, and saffron strands, garnished with crushed almonds. Served warm after crisp mountain air.

The Grand Feast

Wazwan

A multi-course banquet of complex meat preparations like rogan josh and gushtaba, traditionally served from large copper platters shared among family.

When to Go

Each season reshapes the valley's character entirely.

March to May

Spring Blossom

Orchards burst into white and pink blooms; lower valley paths turn vibrant green.

June to August

Summer Meadows

Peak season for high-altitude trekking, alpine wildflowers, and cool lake breezes.

September to November

Golden Autumn

Chinar trees turn glowing copper and amber under crisp, clear photographic skies.

December to February

Winter Snows

Deep powder snow blankets Gulmarg for skiing. Valley vistas turn stark and monochromatic.
